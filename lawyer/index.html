About the Attorney

Adv. Tanvir Rahman

Founding Partner · Trial Attorney

With more than 18 years at the bar, Tanvir Rahman has built a reputation for meticulous preparation and unwavering advocacy. He has represented clients before trial courts, the High Court Division, and arbitral tribunals across a broad range of civil and criminal matters.

His practice is grounded in a simple belief: every client deserves honest advice, clear communication, and a lawyer who fights for the best possible outcome.
